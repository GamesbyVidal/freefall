# Freefall

A browser game where you fall down a shaft and slip through the gaps between ledges.

Play it at https://gamesbyvidal.github.io/freefall/

- Arrow keys on a computer, or touch and slide on a phone
- Every 15 ledges is a new level, and each level is 5% faster
- Every 2 levels you reach a new zone (The Mine Shaft, Crystal Caves, Ice Cavern, Magma Core) and earn its badge
- Collect coins to unlock new characters

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. The numbers that control how it feels are in the SETTINGS section near the top of the script.
